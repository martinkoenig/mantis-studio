# Mantis Studio v0.3 — Geometric Calibration Foundation

Status: Architectural baseline; M0/M1/M2 implemented; M3 and later work deferred

## Milestone intent and compatibility

“From real dual-OV9281 acquisitions, Mantis can deterministically produce a
physically scaled, versioned and validated geometric X1 calibration, activate it,
and bind it reproducibly to future captures and replay.”

The full milestone includes Checkerboard and first-class ChArUco support,
partial ChArUco visibility and stable corner IDs, deterministic diverse datasets,
independent LEFT/RIGHT intrinsic calibration, stereo calibration with fixed
intrinsics, calibrated X1 rig geometry, immutable/versioned artifacts,
project-local activation, exact historical capture/replay references, CLI/C++/
Python controls, a functional Studio calibration workspace and real Q6A acceptance.

Architecture v1 remains frozen. Prefer additive extensions through the existing
base/spatial/calibration, artifact, job, service and client boundaries. The accepted
v0.2 acquisition path is the input foundation; its [acceptance record](milestone.md#v02--real-acquisition-foundation)
does not imply hardware synchronization or validated optical exposure skew.

## Work-package boundary

M0 closes stale v0.2 milestone statements and records this architecture and

## Targets and physical scale

Checkerboard and ChArUco share a planar grid in millimeters. `squares_x` and
`squares_y` are **numbers of squares**, never inner-corner counts. Each axis has
at least two squares so the grid has an interior intersection; later detectors
may require sufficient observations without redefining these counts. A target
carries caller-supplied identity/revision and a typed pattern. ChArUco additionally
requires a nonempty explicit dictionary and a finite positive nominal marker size
strictly smaller than its nominal square. `CharucoDefinition::pattern_layout`
explicitly records physical checkerboard/marker parity: default
`black_square_at_origin`, or `white_square_at_origin_even_rows` for the historical
pre-4.6-compatible even-row pattern. The latter requires even `squares_y`; unknown
layout values are invalid. Layout leaves coordinates and physical scaling unchanged.
A physically valid target may be unsupported by its detector backend: OpenCV 4.6
cannot represent the white-origin layout and returns `Status::incompatible`;
backends exposing `setLegacyPattern()` explicitly select the requested layout.
The preferred physical layout for future owned boards is not frozen.
The preferred dictionary and reference board dimensions are **not frozen**.
M2 maps canonical dictionary names explicitly;
unknown names are errors, with no fallback.

For finite positive nominal square size `s`:

```text
nominal_active_width_mm  = squares_x * s
nominal_active_height_mm = squares_y * s
```

Optional measured active width and height must be present together or absent
together. Measurements are from **outer active-grid edge to opposite outer
active-grid edge**, excluding paper, white margins, mounting plate and substrate.

```text
unmeasured: scale_x = scale_y = 1; source = nominal
measured:   scale_x = active_width_mm  / nominal_active_width_mm
            scale_y = active_height_mm / nominal_active_height_mm
            source = measured

effective_square_pitch_x_mm = s * scale_x
effective_square_pitch_y_mm = s * scale_y
effective_marker_width_mm   = nominal_marker_size_mm * scale_x
effective_marker_height_mm  = nominal_marker_size_mm * scale_y
```

The two scales are independent and never averaged. A 40.0 mm nominal square with
41.3 mm physical pitch in both axes has scales 1.0325/1.0325. Physical pitches
41.3 mm X and 40.6 mm Y instead have scales 1.0325/1.015. Markers then have
independent widths/heights; anisotropic prints are not silently modeled as square.

Optional width/height uncertainty, instrument and note are measurement provenance.
Measured active width/height may exist without detailed provenance. Measurement
provenance may only exist when both measured active extents exist. An empty/default
provenance object still means provenance was supplied and requires both measured
extents; nominal-only targets must have no provenance.
Uncertainties must be finite and non-negative; they establish no confidence or
metrology model in M1. Dimensions and derived scales/pitches must be finite and
positive, including representability in double precision. Invalid arithmetic is
a structural error, not a quality threshold. No maximum scaling error, anisotropy
limit or minimum physical board size is imposed.

Uniform global scale is correctable and global anisotropic X/Y scale is
representable. Two extents do not certify or correct local nonlinear deformation,
shear, warping or curvature. No scanner-accuracy claim follows from this correction.

## Target-local points and observations

The origin is the active-grid outer corner from which columns and rows increase.
+X follows increasing columns, +Y follows increasing rows, +Z is the right-handed
normal (+X cross +Y); board object points have Z=0. Units are millimeters. This
local convention is distinct from camera optical and X1 rig frames.

The M1 primitive scales coordinates about that origin:

```text
effective_x = nominal_x * scale_x
effective_y = nominal_y * scale_y
effective_z = nominal_z
```

It preserves Z and validates finite input/output. M2's executable contracts verify
ChArUco chessboard-corner IDs against native board indices and their planar X/Y
positions. The adapter converts those indices into nominal Mantis target-local
points in double precision and calls this primitive to apply physical scale.
This verified corner mapping does not assert universal equivalence of OpenCV
board coordinate frames with the Mantis-owned physical convention.

Checkerboard and ChArUco feed one TargetObservation: source RawCapture identity,
FrameSet sequence, camera role/identity, point IDs, 2D pixel points, corresponding
3D millimeter target points and detection evidence. Checkerboard IDs are
deterministic detector-grid IDs, **not absolute physical board IDs** across arbitrary
rotation of an unmarked symmetric board. ChArUco retains absolute detector-provided
board corner IDs. Partial ChArUco visibility is valid; LEFT/RIGHT can observe
different sets. Mono uses all accepted
points visible to its camera; stereo uses the intersection of IDs in each stereo
observation, with explicit ID matching rather than array-position correspondence.

## Planned downstream flow

```text
real RawCapture(s)
→ CalibrationTarget
→ detection
→ TargetObservation
→ CalibrationDataset
→ deterministic diverse sample selection
→ LEFT intrinsics
→ RIGHT intrinsics
→ held-out validation
→ stereo solve with fixed intrinsics
→ X1 rig geometry
→ immutable calibration artifacts
→ activation/binding
→ future captures reference exact calibration revision
```

The target is supplied explicitly alongside the recordings, not inferred from
pixels. LEFT and RIGHT mono solves are independent. A dataset may reference
multiple RawCaptures. The same recording inputs, target and analysis configuration
must reproduce the same accepted/selected FrameSet identities. Selection must
favor geometric diversity over hundreds of near-identical 120 FPS frames.
Deterministic ordering/tie-breaking and recorded analysis configuration will be
explicit; numerical selection thresholds are deferred until real data exists.

The camera-model baseline is **pinhole-brown5** with `fx, fy, cx, cy` and
`k1, k2, p1, p2, k3`. Stereo solves extrinsics after mono, holding intrinsics fixed.
No solver or detector is part of M1.

## Planned calibrated coordinate frames

| Frame | Convention |
| --- | --- |
| `org.mantis.camera.left.optical` | +X right, +Y down, +Z forward |
| `org.mantis.camera.right.optical` | +X right, +Y down, +Z forward |
| `org.mantis.x1.rig` | +X right, +Y forward, +Z up |

Transforms use the existing double-precision `T_target_from_source` semantics.
Derive the future rig from calibrated camera geometry, not nominal CAD positions:

- Origin: midpoint between calibrated LEFT and RIGHT camera centers.
- +X: LEFT camera center toward RIGHT camera center.
- +Y: calibrated mean/bisector forward direction, orthogonalized against +X.
- +Z: +X cross +Y.

Future construction must handle degenerate geometry explicitly; no rig
construction or stereo transform code is implemented in M1.

## Planned immutable artifacts and binding

| Future artifact | Version / purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `org.mantis.CalibrationTarget` | v1: exact nominal/measured geometry and provenance |
| `org.mantis.CalibrationDataset` | v1: recording inputs, observations, selection and configuration |
| `org.mantis.CameraCalibration` | v1: one camera's model, solve and validation evidence |
| `org.mantis.RigCalibration` | v1: exact camera revisions, stereo solve and rig transforms |

These are future immutable/versioned artifact types, not M1 persistence codecs.
Future project-local active calibration binding controls subsequent captures.
Historical captures and replay retain exact calibration references/revisions;
changing activation must not reinterpret historical geometry. Artifact encoding,
project migration and service APIs will be separate reviewed work packages.

## Validation philosophy and acceptance limits

Report training, held-out and final-fit metrics separately. Validate repeatability
between independent datasets. Numerical calibration-quality PASS/WARN/FAIL
thresholds are **not frozen**: they require real data. Pixel reprojection RMS is
not scanner accuracy in millimeters and must not yield claims such as “0.03 mm
accuracy.” Physical target scale improves object geometry; it is not metrology
certification. Real Q6A acceptance of the eventual complete calibration workflow
remains part of the full milestone.

Out of scope for all v0.3: laser-plane calibration, laser-line extraction,
triangulation, hardware trigger implementation, tracking, fusion, meshing,
texture generation, scanner metrology certification and accuracy claims derived
from reprojection error. Those do not become implicit extensions of M0/M1.
