## Recommended mathematical contract

Use one original-image observation and one **explicitly supplied declared plane**, evaluated in the calibrated rig frame. This is the smallest single-camera ray/plane method; no stereo correspondence, physical plane estimation, line discovery or Q6A/M8-derived geometry is involved. Ray/plane intersection in the camera frame is equivalent after a checked plane transform; staying in the existing rig frame avoids adding another transform contract. Stereo triangulation needs a second observation and correspondence and is outside this proposal.

All following numerical thresholds and document/result meanings are proposals for review, not accepted architecture or physical validity limits.

**Image and distortion.** Input `source_pixel=(u,v)` is the stored f32 pair widened exactly to binary64: x right, y down, origin at the center of original pixel (0,0), with no half-pixel offset [S8,S10]. It is already in original-source coordinates. Preserve `preprocessing.original_from_processed` and its reference; do not apply it a second time. A producer unable to establish original coordinates is incompatible.

Reuse `PinholeBrown5` and the exact final mono intrinsics in the loaded camera/rig graph [S5,S9,S11]. K has rows `(fx,0,cx)`, `(0,fy,cy)`, `(0,0,1)`, with positive finite focal lengths, no skew, coefficients ordered `k1,k2,p1,p2,k3`. For undistorted normalized `(x,y)`, let `r²=x²+y²`, `a=1+k1 r²+k2 r⁴+k3 r⁶`. Forward distortion is

`xd=x a+2 p1 x y+p2(r²+2x²)`; `yd=y a+p1(r²+2y²)+2 p2 x y`.

Solve these equations for `(xd,yd)=((u-cx)/fx,(v-cy)/fy)`; this is inverse distortion, not applying the forward polynomial to the measured pixel. A bounded inverse policy must declare a convex normalized validity domain D and a positive lower bound γ on the smallest eigenvalue of the symmetric part of its Jacobian throughout D. A conservative coefficient/domain bound can certify strong monotonicity and hence uniqueness; a local determinant or a converged residual alone cannot. Missing/failed certification refuses this model/domain. This domain/certificate is a genuine ALG-03 policy gap: M4 finite, positive-focal calibration validation does not prove global inverse uniqueness.

Propose at most 100 inverse iterations, no unbounded search/retries, a checked iterate in D, and forward reprojection residual ≤1e-9 pixel. Nonconvergence, domain escape, failed Jacobian conditioning or uncertified uniqueness rejects the row. No zero-distortion fallback. These termination settings belong to the separately versioned processing policy; M4 solver settings are not automatically inverse-distortion guarantees. Synthetic D below is `[-0.5,0.5]²`, γ=1 for zero distortion; k1=0.01 with other distortion zero also has Jacobian eigenvalues ≥1 there.

**Frames, units and intersection.** Camera optical axes are +X right, +Y down, +Z forward. Rig axes follow actual M4 construction: +X LEFT→RIGHT, +Y calibrated forward orthogonalized to +X, +Z their cross product; origin is the calibrated camera-center midpoint. Names and IDs must match the supplied graph, rather than assuming X1 frame-name constants [S4–S6]. Reuse row-major double `T_rig_from_camera=[R,t]`: `p_rig=R p_camera+t`, translations/positions in mm, rotation dimensionless, any angles in radians. Validate proper rotation, homogeneous last row, exact frame ID/name and M4 composition; never invert a transform merely to make IDs appear to fit.

Set `h=(x,y,1)`, `w=h/||h||`, `o=t`, `v=R w`. With declared unit plane normal n and signed offset d in mm, plane equation is `n·p+d=0`. Define `s=n·o+d`, `q=n·v`, `τ=-s/q`, and `p=o+τ v`. Thus τ is forward ray distance in mm. Camera depth is `z_camera=τ w_z`. Reject nonfinite intermediate/results, a camera on the plane, parallel/coplanar rays, `τ≤0`, or `z_camera≤0`. No intersection-at-infinity or coplanar arbitrary point is published.

Plane orientation is explicit, not inferred from coefficient sign. Proposed side policy is either `two_sided` or `camera_on_positive_side`; the latter requires `s>0` and `q<0`. Synthetic default uses the latter with n pointing toward the cameras. Reversing n and d under a one-sided policy changes its meaning and requires a distinct declared revision. This “backside” check concerns only the declared oriented plane. Reflecting-object backfaces and physical laser emission volume cannot be determined without additional supplied geometry/evidence; no such claim is made.

**Finite domain and conditioning.** Require original-image `0≤u≤width-1`, `0≤v≤height-1` (a deliberately conservative center-bounded domain, not a new global schema-1 restriction), membership in declared D, and explicit inclusive optical-depth bounds `0<z_min≤z_camera≤z_max`. No clamping, extrapolation or fabricated boundary samples. The synthetic profile uses z in [100,2000] mm, `||p||∞≤2000` mm and `||o||∞≤100` mm. These are software bounds for the reference data, not scanner working distance.

Report `|q|`, `κ_plane=1/|q|`, inverse-Jacobian norm bound, inverse residual, τ and optical depth. Exact `q=0` is parallel; `0<|q|<q_min` is near-parallel. Equality at q_min is eligible only if all other bounds hold. Synthetic `q_min=0.01` gives κ≤100; it is not a universal optical threshold. “Precision” here means numerical representation/conditioning diagnostics, not measured repeatability or calibration accuracy.

For bounded perturbations `|Δs|≤εs`, `|Δq|≤εq<|q|`, a useful conservative distance bound is `|Δτ|≤(εs+|τ| εq)/(|q|-εq)`; position also includes origin/direction perturbations: `||Δp||≤εo+|τ| εv+|Δτ|(1+εv)`. The policy must include floating-point arithmetic margins. f32 coordinate quantization, inverse residual, transform/plane rounding, and any supplied geometry uncertainty have separate contributions. Unknown physical calibration/noise uncertainty stays unknown; a reprojection RMS, ALG-02 curvature or generic confidence is not a covariance or mm error bar [S1,S8,P1].

For the simple exact-pixel zero-distortion references below, propose maximum coordinate error 2e-8 mm against an independent oracle. For distorted references, compare with the forward-generated stored f32 coordinates and their ±0.5-ulp rounding cells, not an unrounded ideal pixel. A future finite bound exceeding its declared numerical allowance rejects the row. Nothing in this document establishes measured mm/µm accuracy or runtime performance.

## Result, refusal and bounded execution proposal

Recommend an internal typed ALG-03 outcome table carried by the existing generic `data::Packet` **Tensor** semantic route and persisted as a new, separately versioned derived geometry artifact. This reuses `Attribute`/BufferView, `SemanticPublished`, `SemanticNodeInstance`, generic Store begin/append/finalize and existing immutable publication [S12–S14]; it does not add a semantic variant, Plugin SDK ABI, global X1 meaning or modify LaserObservation/RAW. The new artifact/table meaning and validator remain **GAPS**, not existing accepted schemas.

One row per source row, in original order: accepted validity, bounded reason bits, applicable diagnostics and f64 `[N,3]` rig-mm position. Rejected positions use finite zero placeholders under a mandatory false validity mask, never usable XYZ. Sample key is observation key plus original index, not compacted position. A nonempty bounded, versioned manifest attribute stores exact source/processing/plane/policy refs, counts/disposition and source lineage once, not repeated heap objects per point; this is proposed artifact payload, not an existing `Packet.context` field. Header frame is the exact rig frame and header calibration is the selected processing-rig logical reference. Derived header time/sync fields use declared neutral values, while the manifest preserves actual source time/evidence and absence. Unknown original observation extensions remain accessible through the immutable referenced source, without reinterpreting them. N=0 retains the manifest and omits all zero-shaped row attributes; a typed validator must distinguish success/no detections, all rejected, extractor failed and extractor unavailable. A malformed or unsupported upstream disposition is never successful empty geometry.

Fix numerical evaluation order and version the backend/arithmetic policy. Derive no payload values from wall clocks, mutable state or random IDs; input publication order is output order. Repeated identical pinned inputs/configuration must reproduce outcomes/provenance. Cross-platform byte identity and arithmetic bounds require future actual qualification; this preparation claims neither. Store-assigned artifact IDs remain outside canonical payload bytes.

Proposed deterministic row gates: producer rejection/ambiguity or unresolved attribution; pixel/FOV domain; inverse failure; parallel/near-parallel/on-plane; side; forward/depth; numerical budget. Preserve all source flags and all applicable independently evaluable reasons; the primary reason is the first failing gate in that order. Do not manufacture dependent diagnostics after a gate makes them undefined. Accepted and rejected counts must sum to N; no sorting, deduplication or silent filtering of valid measurements. A future compact PointCloud view must retain source indices and reference the outcome ledger; it must not expose rejected placeholders as points.

Whole-invocation refusal publishes nothing on malformed buffers/nonfinite source data, unsupported required schema/extension semantics, invalid calibration/plane/configuration, missing exact required references, hash/type/revision/physical-ID/role/dimension/frame/line-to-plane mismatch, conflicting provenance or exhausted capacity. Missing attribution in an otherwise valid row is a row rejection; a declared plane for the wrong requested line is a configuration mismatch. No automatic alternate plane, camera, calibration, backend or algorithm. Unknown flag bits survive; an explicitly required but uninterpretable quality meaning causes incompatible refusal. N=0 still validates context and selected geometry.

Bound work by existing limits (≤1,000,000 input samples, ≤128 attributes, ≤128 MiB semantic payload) and the semantic graph's simultaneous input/output backing budget (default 256 MiB; finite ≤2 GiB) [S10,S13]. Add explicit lower algorithm admission limits as needed; charge output and retained source together before allocation. O(N) rows and fixed inverse cap; use bulk arrays, no per-row allocation/string/retry. Proposed cancellation checks before allocation, between rows/iterations, at least every 256 rows, and before publication; cancellation is a failed invocation with no partial successful artifact. This is a required behavior proposal, not a measured latency bound.

Own pinned geometry/configuration immutably for the node instance; retain source backing through execution and output ownership as required. Reuse serial instance processing and independent instances, deadline/cancellation, lossless bounded streaming and Store durability. Native plugin callbacks remain cooperative; isolation is required for enforceable termination of untrusted code [S13,S14]. No acquisition session, device, IPC, queue owner or background thread is introduced by preparation.

**One decisive contract question:** For the first ALG-03 result, choose **A (recommended)**: the single Tensor-carried typed outcome table above, with validity/XYZ/reasons and one independently reviewed geometry artifact meaning; or **B**: compact existing PointCloud output plus a separately linked complete rejection ledger, requiring an explicit empty-cloud and atomic/multi-output publication contract before implementation. A fits the existing single-result semantic node, zero-dimension prohibition and source-preserving outcomes with fewer new publication rules; B fits direct PointCloud consumption but those ledger/empty-output rules are absent [S12–S14]. This is an output-contract decision for independent geometry review, not generic permission to start.
